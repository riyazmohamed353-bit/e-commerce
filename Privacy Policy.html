Privacy Policy Last updated: September 2026 ReTech AI ("we", "our", "the app") is a marketplace app for buying and selling used electronics. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Information we collect Account information: your name, email address, phone number, and password (stored securely, never in plain text). Location information: your pincode and city, which you provide voluntarily to power location-based search and to help buyers and sellers find listings near them. Listing content: photos, descriptions, prices, and specifications you submit when posting an item for sale. Messages: chat messages exchanged between buyers and sellers through the app, used to facilitate transactions. Usage data: basic technical information such as device type and app version, used for debugging and improving the service. How we use your information To create and manage your account, including email verification via one-time passcodes (OTP). To let you post listings, browse the marketplace, and message other users. To provide AI-powered features (fair price estimates, photo condition analysis, negotiation suggestions, and smart search) using third-party AI services. Listing details and photos you submit may be sent to these services solely to generate these features. To send you transactional emails, such as OTP codes for signup and password resets. To improve app performance and fix bugs. Sharing of information We do not sell your personal information. Your name and listing details are visible to other users of the app as part of normal marketplace functionality (e.g., a seller's name is visible to a buyer they are chatting with). We may share information with service providers who help us operate the app (such as our hosting provider and AI service provider), solely to provide the app's features. Data storage and security Your data is stored on secure third-party infrastructure. Passwords are encrypted. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Your choices You can update your profile information (name, phone, pincode, city) at any time within the app. You can delete individual listings you have posted. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the email below. Children's privacy ReTech AI is not intended for use by children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
